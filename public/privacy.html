Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

In short: Mise stores the food, workout and body information you enter so the app can work. We don’t sell it. You can download or delete it at any time.

Who we are

Mise (“we”, “us”) is a nutrition and training tracking website run by its owner. Contact:

Who can use Mise

Mise is for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 18 we recommend you use it with a parent or guardian’s knowledge. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, contact us and we will delete it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a password (stored by our sign-in provider as a secure hash; we never see your password). If you use Google sign-in, we receive your name and email from Google.
  • Profile information you enter: name, age, height, weight, sex, activity level, goal, diet and allergies.
  • Health and fitness data you enter: foods and calories logged, water, weight and body measurements, workouts, sets, personal records, recipes, meal plans and shopping lists. Some of this (for example weight, age and diet) can be considered health information, so we treat all of it as sensitive.
  • Coach conversations: messages you type or say to the Mise Coach, and photos you choose to send for meal logging.
  • Technical data: basic request information (such as IP address and browser type) that our hosting provider processes to deliver the site and limit abuse.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show your diary, plans, workouts and progress, and calculate your targets.
  • To sync your data between your devices if you create a cloud account.
  • To answer your Coach questions and estimate calories from your text, voice or photos.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it.

Where your data is stored and who processes it

  • Your device: a copy of your data is kept in your browser’s storage. Clearing site data removes it.
  • Supabase (cloud accounts only): stores your email, sign-in and your Mise data in a database protected so that only your account can read it.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the website and the small server that forwards Coach requests.
  • Google (Gemini API): when you use the Coach, photo logging, “Say or type it” or AI planning, the text or photo you send and the relevant context (such as your targets, diet and recent logs) is sent to Google to generate a reply. Please don’t put information in a message that you wouldn’t want processed this way.
  • Your browser’s speech recognition: if you use the microphone button, your browser (for example Chrome or Safari) may send audio to its vendor to convert it to text. This is controlled by your browser, not by Mise.

These providers may process data in countries other than your own.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. You can clear parts of your data or delete your account in Profile, Data. Backups held by our providers may persist for a short period after deletion.

Your choices and rights

  • Download a copy: Profile, Data, Download backup.
  • Correct information: edit it in the app.
  • Delete: Profile, Data, Delete account (and “Delete my cloud data”). You can also email us to ask for deletion.
  • Depending on where you live (for example Australia, the UK/EU or California) you may have additional rights to access, correct or delete personal information and to complain to your privacy regulator. Contact us and we will help.

Security

Connections use HTTPS and cloud data is protected by account-level access rules. No online service is perfectly secure, so use a strong, unique password.

Cookies and local storage

Mise uses your browser’s local storage to keep you signed in and save your data on your device. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Changes

If we make important changes we will update this page and the date above.
